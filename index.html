Who am I?

What's the Funk-o-meter? 🎯

Every coffee has a dial that tells you how wild the cup gets, before you even brew.

Blue = Classic. Clean, elegant and comforting: caramel and chocolatey cups that work beautifully with milk, plus crisp Kenyans, floral Geishas and the timeless greats.

Red = Funky. Wild fermentations, tropical fruit-bombs, boozy, lactic, candy-shop chaos that makes you go “wait… that’s coffee?!”

Neither end is better, it’s just your mood. Chase the red for adventure, stay blue for a reliable classic.
